Adunbox 6H Final Model Selection

Chosen production candidate:
- target-wise LightGBM ensemble over raw 6h metrics
- source data: F:\adunbox_joined_traffic_reports_with_timezone.csv
- training rows are valid anchor windows, not raw CSV rows
- anchor count: 51,659
- train rows: 10,871
- validation rows: 12,638
- test rows: 28,150

Target routing:
- spend -> anchor_v2
- impressions -> anchor_v2
- clicks -> anchor_v2
- conversions -> business_v3
- revenue -> business_v3

Selected final test R2:
- spend: 0.357898
- impressions: 0.424732
- clicks: 0.495425
- conversions: 0.423132
- revenue: 0.316992

Decision note:
- anchor_v2 improved impressions and clicks substantially and kept spend nearly unchanged
- business_v3 improved conversions and revenue substantially
- because production requires raw metrics, the selected candidate routes each raw target to the stronger LightGBM model
- revenue remains the weakest metric, so production should use confidence flags, benchmark fallback, and spike caps
